### Mariane El Kassis

**Researcher** · PhD in Modelling and Simulation (2024)

I work on **business process simulation and AI**:
turning business process models into executable discrete-event simulations, making heterogeneous
simulators interoperate, and evaluating how AI changes the performance of real processes, as a basis
for process optimisation.

**Research interests:** business process modelling and simulation (BPMN, BPSim) · DEVS ·
High Level Architecture (HLA) · model-driven engineering and metamodelling · simulation-based
evaluation of AI-enabled processes
